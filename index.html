Frequently asked questions

How does payment protection work?

Your payment is held in escrow. After delivery, the publisher’s share waits 20 days. If you report a problem, it stays on hold until fixed. If you say nothing, it is released after 20 days; approving earlier releases it at once.

Who writes the article?

Either side. Submit your own text or choose publisher writing, which adds a fixed fee to the order.

What does GPlinx charge?

A flat 10% from the buyer and 10% from the publisher on each order.

Can buyers and publishers see each other’s names?

No. Conversations run on order numbers, and phone numbers, emails and links are hidden automatically.

How do I list my website?

Create a publisher account, add your site, verify ownership, and wait for admin approval.

Is the site available in other languages?

Yes. The platform is built for many languages, and more are added over time.
