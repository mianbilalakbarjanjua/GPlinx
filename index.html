Frequently asked questions

How does payment protection work?

Your payment is held in escrow. After delivery, the publisher’s share waits 20 days. If you report a problem, it stays on hold until fixed. If you say nothing, it is released after 20 days; approving earlier releases it at once.

Who writes the article?

Either side. Submit your own text or choose publisher writing, which adds a fixed fee to the order.

What does GPlinx charge?

A flat 10% from the buyer and 10% from the publisher on each order.

Can buyers and publishers see each other’s names?

No. Conversations run on order numbers, and phone numbers, emails and links are hidden automatically.

How do I list my website?

Create a publisher account, add your site, verify ownership, and wait for admin approval.

Is the site available in other languages?

Yes. The platform is built for many languages, and more are added over time.

Terms of Service

Last updated: 16 November 2026

1. What GPlinx is

GPlinx is an online marketplace where advertisers (buyers) order guest posts and links on websites owned by publishers. We provide the platform, hold payments in escrow and help resolve problems. We do not own the listed websites and we do not promise any ranking, traffic or search engine result.

2. Your account

You must be at least 18 years old and give true information. Keep your password safe: you are responsible for what happens in your account. We may suspend or close accounts that break these terms or put other users at risk.

3. Prices and fees

Publishers set their own prices in US dollars. The buyer pays the publisher's price plus a flat 10% service fee, plus a fixed extra fee if the buyer asks the publisher to write the article. The publisher receives the price minus a flat 10% fee. We may change our fees; a change only applies to orders placed after it is announced.

4. Adding money to your wallet

You can add funds with the payment methods shown on the Add funds page (for example PayPal, USDT, bank transfer, JazzCash, Easypaisa or a card payment page). Send the payment to the details shown there, then enter the transaction or reference number; you can also upload a picture of your payment slip. Your wallet is credited after we confirm the payment. Always use the network shown for USDT: money sent on a different network or to a wrong address cannot be recovered. A transaction ID can be used only once. If a payment is reversed, cancelled or found to be fraudulent, we may remove the credit and suspend the account.

5. How an order works

When a buyer orders, the payment is held in escrow. The publisher accepts the order, the article is written (by the buyer or the publisher, as chosen), and the publisher delivers the live link within the promised time. If the publisher misses the delivery time, the buyer can wait longer or take a full refund to their wallet.

6. Review period and payment release

After delivery, the publisher's payment is held for 20 days. During this time the buyer can report a problem. While a problem is open, the payment stays on hold until the publisher fixes it, and then a new 20-day period starts. If the buyer approves, or if 20 days pass with no open problem, the publisher's payment is released automatically. Publishers should keep delivered links live during the review period; removing a link during that time is a reportable problem.

7. Publisher payouts

Publishers can withdraw their available balance (minimum $10) to the payout details (PayPal, USDT, bank account or mobile wallet) saved in their settings. Payouts are reviewed and sent manually. You are responsible for the accuracy of your payout details; payments sent to wrong details cannot be recovered.

8. Rules

You may not use GPlinx for anything illegal, deceptive or harmful. This includes scams, malware, hate content, adult content, copyright infringement, fake or spam websites, and misleading claims. Content on sensitive topics (such as crypto, finance or health claims) may only be ordered from websites that accept them. You may not try to take deals outside the platform by sharing contact details in chat (these are hidden automatically), abuse refunds or chargebacks, or create several accounts to cheat.

9. Disputes

If buyer and publisher disagree, contact us. We will review the order, the messages and the link in good faith and may release the payment, refund the buyer or keep the funds on hold. Our decision on how funds held on GPlinx are paid out is final.

10. Affiliate program

If you refer a buyer with your personal link, you earn 5% of the platform fees on that buyer's completed orders. The commission is added to your wallet balance. Self-referrals and abuse are not allowed, and we may cancel commissions earned unfairly.

11. Our responsibility

GPlinx is provided "as is". We are not responsible for search engine penalties, ranking changes, or the actions of users. To the extent the law allows, our total responsibility for any order is limited to the fees we received for that order.

12. Changes and contact

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

What we collect

Account details (name, email, country, phone if you add it), billing or payout details you choose to save (such as a PayPal email or a USDT wallet address), the websites you list, your orders, messages and delivered links, your username (made from your email), an optional profile picture, payment slips you choose to upload, payment records (amounts and transaction IDs), and basic technical data needed to keep the service secure.

Why we use it

To run your account, process orders, hold and release payments, confirm top-ups, send payouts, prevent fraud, resolve disputes, send service emails (such as account confirmation and password reset), and meet legal and accounting duties. We do not sell your personal data and we do not show advertising.

What other users can see

Buyers and publishers do not see each other's real names or contact details. They do see each other's username (the part of the email before the @, which cannot be changed) and the optional profile picture, so please use an email address you are comfortable with. Order chat runs on order numbers, and phone numbers, emails and links are hidden automatically. Payment slips are stored privately: only you and the GPlinx administrators can open them. Buyers see a listed website's details (domain, niche, price and quality figures) but not the owner's identity.

Who helps us run the service

We use trusted providers: Supabase (database, login and file storage), GitHub (hosting of this website), and an email delivery service for service emails. When you pay by PayPal or USDT, the payment network handles the payment and we receive the transaction details. These providers process data only to provide their service to us.

Cookies and browser storage

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser stores a login session so you stay signed in; logging out removes it.

How long we keep data

While your account is active, and afterwards as long as needed for accounting, fraud prevention and legal reasons.

Security

The site uses an encrypted connection. Access to data is limited by account rules, and administrator actions are logged. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data by writing to support@gplinx.com. We may keep records we are required to keep. GPlinx is not for people under 18.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.

Refund Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Orders

If the publisher does not deliver within the promised time, you can take a full refund (the price and the service fee) to your GPlinx wallet, or choose to wait longer. If a delivered link has a problem, report it within the 20-day review period: the payment stays on hold until the publisher fixes it. If it is not fixed, contact us and we will review the order and may refund you.

Completed orders

Once you approve an order, or the 20-day review period ends with no open problem, the payment is released to the publisher and the order is final.

Wallet balance

Money added to your wallet is meant for orders on GPlinx. If you want unused balance returned, write to us and we will review the request.

Payouts

If we reject a payout request, the amount returns to your wallet balance.

How to ask

Write to support@gplinx.com with your order number. Refunds to your wallet appear in your balance; we explain any other refund before sending it.
